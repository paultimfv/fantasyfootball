The rulebook

Points per unit in each format. Your friends' league uses one of these, possibly with tweaks. The only difference between formats is catches.

Rule of thumb: targets and carries repeat week to week, touchdowns don't. A player whose points are mostly orange had a lucky week. Mostly green means the opportunity is real.
